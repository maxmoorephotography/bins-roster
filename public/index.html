How the roster works

  • Two townhouses on duty each week, rotating through all 5 so every household takes an equal share over time.
  • Rostering two people is deliberate: if one is away or forgets, the other still gets the bins out — a minimum of two bins reliably makes it to the kerb.
  • Green food & garden bin goes out every week — it's collected weekly.
  • Red or yellow general bins alternate week to week with the council's fortnightly collection.
  • Bins go to the kerb Wednesday afternoon/evening and come back any time Thursday, after the 7am collection.
  • On weeks with a public holiday that shifts council collection, your calendar reminder will flag it — always worth a quick check with council if unsure.
  • Subscribe your unit's calendar below and reminders arrive automatically — no email address needed, and nothing breaks if your email changes.
